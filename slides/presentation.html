4The setup

2048 is a game against luck.
So we made the luck fight back.

After every move a new tile appears at random: a 2 (90%) or a 4 (10%). We replace that randomness with an AI.

2Villain

Tries every cell and value, predicts your reply two moves ahead, places the worst one.

4Random

The normal game, exactly.

2Helper

Same adversarial search, places the best tile instead.

Against the villain

Same checkpoints, same 100 games

From 100 to 1,000,000 training games, the bot's normal-game score grew 6.9× (5,917 → 40,585). Against the villain it went from 578 to 686 (1.2×).

23 minutes to train 1,000,000 games on a laptop (TD learning of an n-tuple network, Szubert & Jaskowski 2014, implemented from scratch). Bot and villain are deterministic: 10–18 distinct villain games per checkpoint.
